Delete Your Account

Operated by Karl Mumm (sole trader), Auckland, New Zealand  ·  admin@psle-ace.com  ·  Last updated: 29 July 2026

You, or a parent/guardian on behalf of a child under 13, can request deletion of a PSLE Ace account and its associated data at any time. This applies whether the account was created with an email address, or with Google Sign-In.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to admin@psle-ace.com with the subject line "Delete My PSLE Ace Account".
  2. Include the email address or username associated with the account, so we can locate it. If you are a parent or guardian requesting deletion of a child's account, please let us know this in your message.
  3. We will verify the request and confirm deletion by email within 10 business days.

Email us to delete your account

What Gets Deleted

Once a deletion request is confirmed, the following data is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days:

  • Your account credentials (email address, login information)
  • Display name and profile photo (if set)
  • Quiz statistics, scores, and activity history
  • Notification preferences
  • Any anonymous guest progress data linked to your device, if applicable

What May Be Retained

We may retain limited information after deletion where required by law or legitimate business need, specifically:

  • Subscription and payment records — Google Play or the Apple App Store retain transaction records independently of us, in accordance with their own policies, for tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention purposes. We do not control this retention.
  • Aggregated, de-identified usage data that can no longer be linked back to you or your child, which we may retain to understand overall app performance.

We do not retain any data that could be used to identify you or your child beyond what is described above.

Deleting Only Some of Your Data

If you don't want to delete your entire account, but want us to delete or correct specific information (for example, a profile photo, or display name), you can request this the same way — email admin@psle-ace.com and describe what you'd like changed or removed. We will respond within 10 business days.

Questions

If you have any questions about this process, or about how your data is handled, see our Privacy Policy or contact us at admin@psle-ace.com.
